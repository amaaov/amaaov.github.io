# Existence

**Published:** 27 August 2026, Helsinki
**Modified:** 1 September 2026
**Author:** amaaov
**Language:** English · [Русский](20260827165000_existence_ru.md)
**HTML:** [English](20260827165000_existence_en.html) · [Русский](20260827165000_existence.html)

---

The lamp is already on when I notice the hour, and on the floor the five have stopped, objects that were supposed to stay in the air and did not, round, indifferent, ready to be picked up or left. I have been throwing, and then not throwing, and I have not yet named what this was. Indoors the sound is small, a drop, a roll, Helsinki outside in the usual late light, and I could put the objects in the bag and go, because nobody is waiting for a clip of this. I still feel the pattern that failed, a crossing, a height that asked for a fifth object before the fourth had come home, and I sit on the floor with my back to the wall and look at them, so I could pick them up again, or leave them and let the hour thin.

When a transition appears that my hands cannot keep, I sometimes leave a note or a crude recording, something incomplete that another person could start from. One body need not discover the movement, perform it, record it, and explain the whole evening. Printing houses, bands, laboratories, and workshops have long divided such routes among people. What presses now is the opposite demand: that one name hold the route together and one public file prove the person behind it still exists as a candidate. I have felt that demand in the hands that drop the five.

I have left files where a stranger can find them: source code, a phrase, a failed mechanism, a photograph whose sequence I have not chosen. Publishing in this mood means leaving a drawer open. Someone may extract, contradict, repair, fork, perform, or misunderstand what is there, and I may never hear about it. That distance lets the contribution remain partial and combinable.

If authenticity still helps, it lies in a trace faithful to its conditions. This drop happened; this recording is what the air held; these are the conditions I remember. A polished sequence can lie about its arrival while a failed five tells the truth of an hour. Skill, difficulty, usefulness, and beauty run on other axes. The trace can be faithful even though no single person was ever the whole stack.

A clinic makes the separation sharper. Under California Family Code §7613, a sperm donor who provides semen to a licensed physician or sperm bank for assisted reproduction by someone other than the donor’s spouse is ordinarily not treated as a parent unless a written agreement before conception says otherwise ([California Family Code §7613](https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=FAM&sectionNum=7613.)). Identity is a separate question. In the United Kingdom, people conceived from donors who donated at licensed clinics on or after 1 April 2005 can request identifying details at eighteen; the first cohort began reaching that age in October 2023, while transitional exceptions remain ([HFEA](https://www.hfea.gov.uk/donation/donors/faqs-for-donors-donor-conceived-people-and-their-parents/)). Commercial DNA matching can identify donors outside the register. The analogy has a limit: genes are not a sentence, a rhythm, or source code, and a file can be copied without being consumed. Still, the vulgar word cuts through prestige. A contributor need not contain the whole organism or remain the parent of what grows.

A quiet drawer stores material until someone wants it. A feed does more as it delivers: selecting, cropping, captioning, sequencing, and making another reason to look. Supply can multiply without enlarging anyone’s attention, so the fertility that enriches a commons also cheapens extraction. I have published fragments while knowing both. Once the file exists, a next begins to form in the person who left it: someone may use this, therefore I should maintain it, answer for it, watch it, return. The pull toward descendants tries to settle over the evening while the five are still on the floor.

The five on the floor happened; putting them in the bag will not undo the hour. “You did this before” names part of its history. “You owe a next now” adds a claim on the present. Someone will later ask whether I still do this. The question can carry warmth, or it can arrive holding a clip, a credit line, a continuation, a name that must keep being that name; the drop alone establishes none of those. The past is irreversible; a debt can be revised. Claims are abandoned, people release one another from promises, relationships stop keeping accounts. The drop stays while the demand for a next can go. A signed loan installed that demand at the start; this evening did not.

Kropotkin’s person sees a neighbour’s house on fire and runs with a pail, moved by solidarity wider than love for someone he may not know ([Kropotkin, 1902](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/4341)). The act does not by itself write a future return into a ledger. I have carried a prop, a file, or a spare part toward other people in that spirit. The carrying becomes debt only when we treat a return as binding and still recognise it now. Marcel Mauss described gift systems in which giving, receiving, and repayment already bind, and the thing given remains attached to an obligation to return ([Mauss, 1925](https://archive.org/details/giftformsfunctio00maus)). When I leave a deposit open and hope for continuation without retaining parenthood, I am trying to stop before the third obligation. That is the pull I still feel at the drawer.

Need interrupts this freedom to revise. A meal still has to be made for someone who cannot make it. Refusing the ledger does not feed them, and Silvia Federici keeps unpaid housework visible as work ([Federici, 1975](https://theanarchistlibrary.org/library/silvia-federici-wages-against-housework)). Yet need does not decide that one body must carry every hour of care; care can be shared, arranged, and supported ([Beier, 2026](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/democratic-theory/article/times-of-caring-democracy/7503AB1FFF0484E1C2B8B4F3730F28D0)). That freedom begins only where a person can stop without transferring harm to someone else.

Friendship, a jam, or an affinity group can fill unpaid hours and still expect someone to show up, sustain the gathering, or return with a next. Those hours may be dear and still lay a claim on what follows. An artefact can exist without such a claim: a file in a drawer, a five that was thrown. Continuation debt is the live contradiction inside an hour that can end.

Research on temporal autonomy offers a plain neighbour. Robert Goodin and his collaborators call time beyond life’s necessary functions “discretionary”: time a person can spend as they please, including on more paid or unpaid work ([Goodin et al., 2008](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511611452.003)). That account leaves the content open but still measures a reserve outside necessity. Here one hour is enough to ask what claim it leaves behind.

An hour like this has already happened. The person living it can stop without making the unfinished part a failure; the hour lays no claim of result, progress, audience, identity, or continuation on what comes next. In Helsinki I carry [*olemassaolo*](https://www.kielitoimistonsanakirja.fi/#/olemassaolo) in my mouth, existence, and hear in its final *olo* how being here feels. Respect existence and expect resistance. Here *and* holds respect and refusal together: a person may decline the next task, a body may refuse the movement asked of it, and a fragment may remain unfit for a future someone planned. After the drop I may lie on the floor or send the five up again; both belong to the hour. Pay may already be part of the week, and a professional can still spend an hour this way. The hour may produce something or contain three ugly hours of a movement that will not arrive. Purpose and becoming can pass through without taking possession of it. A plan I can drop belongs in such an hour; a plan I cannot leave has already claimed the next.

Theodor Adorno writes that interviewers repeatedly asked about his hobby. He answered that he had none: making and listening to music and reading attentively were parts of his life, and the hobby label mocked their seriousness. The ordinary question assumes everyone must have one ([Adorno, 1969](https://xenopraxis.net/readings/adorno_freetime.pdf)). It still arrives over the five.

Giorgio Agamben comes close from another direction. In inoperativity, a work does not have to consume the potential that made it; potential and habit can remain present and open to another use ([Agamben, 2016](https://doi.org/10.1515/9780804798617-025)). He is thinking about form-of-life and *opera*. On the floor, the five can remain until morning, and the file in the drawer is the other weather of the same evening. Tiziana Terranova describes online free labor as voluntarily given, unwaged, enjoyed, and exploited at once ([Terranova, 2000](https://doi.org/10.1215/01642472-18-2_63-33)). Her warning reaches the quiet drawer: a commons can be captured as unpaid production. Enjoyment, pay, usefulness, and publication therefore cannot decide whether an hour claims what comes next.

If I throw toward a path where a stranger walks, my freedom to stop has already narrowed; harm and consent decide how light the evening can remain. A jam can dissolve, a kitchen can be cleaned later, a hall can empty. When stopping would cause irreversible harm to someone who did not agree, or leave care without a safe handover, the exit closes. The same hour can still include access to a craft, a junior task, a body learning. A politics of becoming needs the right not to be recruited, even by that promise of growth.

Hunger, the threat of losing shelter, and unsafe surroundings can drain even hours left free on paper. Security changes what a body can do with time. When bread no longer depends on being called back to a task, remaining still becomes a real choice; policies of forced activation treat the person as a fault to be corrected.

I pick up the jacket, and the five can go in the bag or stay on the floor until morning, either way they have already happened.

On the tram my body is tired while the window keeps moving. Nobody is owed a clip of the evening, and the file I may have left in a drawer need not parent whatever comes from it. Debt was the hinge between those two facts.
