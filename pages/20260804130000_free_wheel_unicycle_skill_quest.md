This is an independent programme, unaffiliated with IUF / USA skill levels. IUF glide and coast cards are fixed-gear freestyle, and freewheel grades here stay separate. Freewheel falls can injure, so wear a helmet and pads, use soft ground, and treat posture cues as one route among several.

## Charter

Grades are cumulative riding under freewheel constraints. Still sitting with the brake locked against a wall is a setup drill, and seals begin when the freewheel is in motion. Early mounts stay static with the brake on (one pedal down, one up). Every grade after the gate mixes scrubbing with the brake and floating in a coast. Posture passes when the back stays straight, forward lean means shoulders forward, and any backward seat correction lives only in the butt. Braking practice starts with shoulders already forward and keeps them forward through pulses and stops, because shoulders back into the lever is a heavy, hard-to-control dump. Early grades may keep the lever busy; later grades lengthen brakeless coast and pedal stretches as the hand relaxes. Stand-jump and rolling-jump mounts belong in FW3.

- Pass prior grades before claiming the next seal.
- Declare wheel size, hub, brake, and surface when attesting.
- Checks stay on this device (localStorage); export a certificate when a grade completes.
- Fail reasons belong in your notes: rounded back on stop, late lever on the down, shoulders back into the brake, upright release into a backward dump.

## Entry gate — fixed + setup

### Fixed gear

- G0.1 Solid fixed-gear mount, dismount, flat and gentle hills until looking around is boring.
- G0.2 Hand-brake feathering on fixed while pedaling feels ordinary.
- G0.3 Gliding attempt on fixed (feet on frame or pegs, wheel rolling).
- G0.4 Brake-assisted coast attempt on fixed (feet off pedals; soft downhill OK).

### Setup drill (warm-up)

- G0.5 Supported static mount with the brake fully engaged: settle weight into the seat, locate the raised pedal and lever by feel, then dismount cleanly.

Fail G0.1–G0.4 and stay on fixed. Use G0.5 as warm-up every freewheel session.

## FW1 — Start

- FW1.1 Static brake-on mount (down pedal / up pedal); shoulders-forward lean set before motion; release brake into the first revolution with that lean held (support OK for the mount).
- FW1.2 Ride 25–50 m on flat or slight downhill; back straight, shoulders stay forward.
- FW1.3 Controlled stop with shoulders still forward through the scrub; any backward seat shift only through the butt; clean step-off or dismount.
- FW1.4 Coast ≥10 m hands off the lever. A short light brake-coast may bridge while free-coast forms; free-coast ≥10 m must land before the seal.
- FW1.5 Assisted acceleration into coast: wall push, partner shove, or short run-up, then free coast without panicking the first metres.
- FW1.6 Practiced clear throw-exit once if a backward tip wins (safe exit).

Pass when starts and early stops keep shoulders forward (shoulders back into the brake often ends in a heavy dump) and a short free coast exists.

## FW2 — Brake–coast

- FW2.1 Same static brake-on mount unassisted on flat (down pedal / up pedal; no wall).
- FW2.2 Pulse-brake stretch with shoulders forward, then coast ≥20 m with lever quiet.
- FW2.3 Full stop without dismounting, shoulders still forward into the scrub (backward seat shift only through the butt); regain balance; continue (≥3 linked cycles).
- FW2.4 Continuous ride ≥100 m flat, alternating pedal and coast.
- FW2.5 Tall posture and shoulders-forward lean held through the stop–start cycles.
- FW2.6 Pedal cranks backward while the wheel rolls forward (clutch free); keep balance and line.
- FW2.7 Hold at least three crank clock positions (e.g. horizontal, near-vertical, mid-angle) for a few metres each while coasting or light-pedaling.

Pass when brake, coast, and free-crank feel belong to the same ride, braking keeps the shoulders forward, and the lever may stay busy early then quiet into longer coasts.

## FW3 — Continuous

- FW3.1 Outing ≥500 m with honest coast segments.
- FW3.2 First stand-jump or rolling-jump mount, after the static brake-on mount is already boring.
- FW3.3 Gentle uphill and downhill; feathering early on the down.
- FW3.4 Firm stop from moderate speed, stay mounted, then find remount cadence.
- FW3.5 Crank flip while braking. Scrub with the lever, flip cranks ~180° to a new foot position, and keep the stop or slow-roll controlled.
- FW3.6 Uphill restore. On a gentle climb, let forward drive fade into roll-back or stall, then re-engage forward pedaling and resume without a panic step-off.

Pass when the brake hand is boring and uphill fade does not end the outing.

## FW4 — Terrain & options

### Required

- FW4.1 Undulating path or pumptrack-style transitions without stepping off every crest.
- FW4.2 Intentional small hop or hop-stop.
- FW4.3 Coast ≥50 m in one float (declare surface).
- FW4.4 Remount after a chosen tip or step-off without walking the uni home as the default.

### Electives (at least one)

- FW4.E1 Extreme stop, stay mounted, continue.
- FW4.E2 Trail freewheel segment with a declared brake plan.
- FW4.E3 Uphill backward attempt, then restore clean forward travel (advanced).

Seal FW4 when all required tasks are checked and at least one elective is logged.
