I've been looking for a strict definition of juggling and for its difference from object manipulation, psychology, subcultures, and identity staying outside this frame. The turn was seeing that, at a single moment, the state is two at once: something held and something already unheld. Hold and akrateia are quantities measured here, and from them come four states that pass into one another.

The system I look at is body, objects, and environment. Body here is what retains: a palm, a pinch, a press against the chest, a ball parked on a foot, several people who share the same objects, a robot. An object is the stillest member, almost never changing itself, held or unheld. Environment is the noisiest and is outside the body's command: gravity and air in flight, floor and wall in bounce, a strike that only collides, a draught, an uneven ball. Kratos and akrateia are read on the objects the body holds or that are already unheld. Environment acts more strongly on what is already unheld and increases the uncertainty of each trajectory, the entropy of the system.

## Definitions

Let attention $O(t)$ be the finite physical objects in an environment. An object belongs to it while there is focus, control or potential control, and an expectation that the body will take it again. Each object in $O$ is either bodily held or not. Held means physical retention by some part of the body; remote influence does not count.

For object $i$, $b_i(t)$ is $1$ if the body holds it now and $0$ if it is unheld.

$$b_i(t)\in\{0,1\}$$

The state of the attention $S(t)$ is two answers at once: whether any object is unheld now, and whether any is held.

$$S(t)=(\exists i\,\neg b_i(t),\,\exists i\,b_i(t))$$

From those two answers come four signs: $\emptyset$, $\kappa$, $\alpha$, $\alpha\kappa$.

Object count and hand count do not enter. With $n=|O|$ and $h=\sum_i b_i$, the sign already follows: $\emptyset$ when $n=0$, $\kappa$ when $h=n>0$, $\alpha\kappa$ when $0\lt h\lt n$, $\alpha$ when $h=0\lt n$. Empty attention is $\emptyset$. Total hold is $\kappa$: object manipulation, every object in $O$ retained. Total akrateia is $\alpha$. $\alpha\kappa$ is hold and akrateia at once. Juggling keeps $\alpha\kappa$ and may briefly enter $\alpha$. Total akrateia cannot be held for long. If a ball on the floor is brought into attention while something is already held, the system is in $\alpha\kappa$ while nothing is happening: the signs still need an expectation of return. A drop is when that expectation breaks.

Siteswap, as Juggling Lab states it, records which hand will next throw an object and how many beats will elapse first; dwell, vacant time, and whether another object remains held while one is airborne belong to a later physical reading ([Juggling Lab](http://jugglinglab.org/html/ssnotation.html)). Chung and Graham’s siteswap *state* is a description of landings, a $1$ at a position meaning some airborne object will land in that many beats, and the number of $1$s being the number of balls ([Chung and Graham, 2008](https://fanchung.ucsd.edu/wp/pjs.pdf)). In $S$ it is visible whether bodily possession is present now, and whether akrateia is present in the same attention.

## Examples

Empty attention is only in $\emptyset$.

A system of one object is only in $\kappa$ and in $\alpha$. The throw path goes $\kappa\rightarrow\alpha\rightarrow\kappa$.

A system of two objects is in $\kappa$, in $\alpha$, and in $\alpha\kappa$. Then one object is held and the other is unheld:

$$\exists i\,b_i \land \exists i\,\neg b_i$$

Usually $x$ and $y$ are different objects. From $\alpha\kappa$ a catch or release goes at once into $\kappa$ or $\alpha$. Both unheld at once go $\alpha\kappa\rightarrow\alpha\rightarrow\alpha\kappa$.

A system of three objects is in $\kappa$, in $\alpha$, and in $\alpha\kappa$, and can stay in $\alpha\kappa$ while the held objects change.

Under the hold convention, $02$ is a one-object system that remains in $\kappa$ throughout, Juggling Lab already naming $2$ as a count the hand is free to interpret as a hold, and $0$ as an empty beat. A three-object cascade can remain $\alpha\kappa$ while identities change. A finite $55500$ flash of three objects from rest walks holding $3\to 2\to 1\to 0\to 1\to 2\to 3$, so the sign walks $\kappa\rightarrow\alpha\kappa\rightarrow\alpha\rightarrow\alpha\kappa\rightarrow\kappa$.

Two people sharing one attention are still one body for these $b_i$. One person holding every object while the other has every object in the air already puts the shared system in $\alpha\kappa$.

## Simulator

The same siteswap can take more than one holding trajectory. A $2$ may be read as a hold; in Juggling Lab siteswap the $T$ modifier marks that the same $2$ may be thrown, and that throw brings the system into akrateia. The synchronous cycle ([44],4)(0,0)([22],2) throws all three, passes empty hands, and holds all three again. Another synchronous cycle walks from three in the air into a hold and back to three.

## Theories

Take finite attention in which each object is either held by the body or already unheld. The path moves when one object is released or caught. Incomplete knowledge lives outside $S$ as a separate observation mark. Belnap’s four values *neither / one / the other / both* are the same lattice shape used for incomplete and conflicting information ([Belnap, 1977](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-94-010-1161-7_2)); the signs belong to physical hold and akrateia.

$\alpha\kappa$ is a nontrivial partition of the attention into hold and akrateia, so it needs $n\ge 2$. $S$ carries no cardinalities, so a three-object system is in $\alpha\kappa$ at both $h=1$ and $h=2$. Siteswap is a description of throw and hold rhythm; these are two state spaces, rhythm $T$ and present holding $S$. There is no general map from a siteswap to a holding trajectory, and many siteswaps can share one. The combined reading is $(T,S)$.

Independent subsystems compose by disjunction:

$$S(A\cup B)=S(A)\lor S(B)$$

$\emptyset$ is the identity of that join; $\alpha\kappa$ absorbs. The body itself can be a complex system: one person, several persons, hands as parts, a robot with real objects in the same environment.

Take two people, or several. Separate attentions join by disjunction: two cascades give $\alpha\kappa$, two total holds give $\kappa$. Shared attention is one body for these $b_i$; a pass is a release and a catch on the same $O$, and the object is unheld in flight. Who threw and who caught is not visible in $S$. The number of hands grows; the sign stays the same.

$S$ is visible even without physical objects in attention, whether a picture of balls, a game, or this court. The signs are read where retention and environment are present. The theory becomes applied when $(T,S)$ and the dwell ratio are a condition a path can fail. The path is checked on the court, in an animator, on a robot, in rehearsal, and in a lesson. The score of a piece can keep $\alpha\kappa$ in a cascade, walk $\alpha$ in a three-object flash, gather two people into one attention, or leave each their own. Whoever teaches or talks about the piece watches whether hold is present and whether akrateia is present; dwell decides whether $3$ stays in $\alpha\kappa$.

For $n\ge 2$, any path from total hold to total akrateia passes through $\alpha\kappa$: $\kappa\rightarrow\alpha\kappa\rightarrow\alpha$, and back the other way. Reaching $\alpha$ from $\kappa$ takes at least $n$ releases before a reacquisition. An episode that starts and ends in $\kappa$ on the same $n$ objects has as many catches as throws; a drop is a trajectory without return. From $S$ alone, activity and rest look the same, which is why the expectation of return belongs with the attention.

For an alternating two-hand three-object cascade $3$, the hands are offset by half a hand cycle $C=D+V$, so at least one hand remains occupied when $D\ge V$, or $r=D/(D+V)\ge 1/2$, while at least one ball stays in akrateia. Continuous $\alpha\kappa$ is then $3$ together with that dwell. Beek and Turvey found $r$ prominent at $3/4$, $2/3$, and $5/8$ for three balls, and about $3/4$ for five and seven ([Beek and Turvey, 1992](https://doi.org/10.1037/0096-1523.18.4.934)); the 2024 robot paper summarises human preference near $r\approx 0.7$. If $r$ drops below one half, the cascade itself begins to enter $\alpha$ in the vacant gaps. Shannon’s uniform-juggle theorem supplies the mean held count under the same assumptions ([Shannon, 1993](https://ccom.uprrp.edu/~humberto/shannons-juggling-theorem.html)). Here $F$, $D$, and $V$ are flight, dwell, and vacant beats, $B$ the balls, $H$ the hands:

$$\frac{F+D}{V+D}=\frac{B}{H},\qquad \bar h=Hr$$

Three is the first count at which the partition can keep changing while $S$ stays $\alpha\kappa$. With two objects a catch or release takes the system out of $\alpha\kappa$ through total hold or total akrateia. With three, catch and release walk a cycle of which objects are held:

$$\{1\}\to\{1,2\}\to\{2\}\to\{2,3\}\to\{3\}\to\{3,1\}$$

The cascade’s visible stillness is that exchange inside one sign. Assignments of $\alpha\kappa$ number $2^n-2$, and only one vertex each for $\kappa$ and $\alpha$. For $n\ge 3$ any $\alpha\kappa$ assignment reaches any other $\alpha\kappa$ assignment by single-object events that remain $\alpha\kappa$. A five-ball cascade can remain $\alpha\kappa$ while its skill cost sits on another axis: macrostate topology versus microstate complexity.

Total akrateia is $\alpha$: every object in $O$ is unheld at once. Shannon treated staying in $\alpha$ continuously as a mathematical limit of zero dwell. Robotics studies impact systems on that limit, where force arrives in pulses ([Sanfelice, Teel, and Sepulchre, 2007](https://hybrid.soe.ucsc.edu/sites/default/files/preprints/17.pdf); [Ruggiero, Lippiello, and Siciliano, 2018](https://doi.org/10.1109/lra.2018.2801939); [Ramadani et al., 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06704)). Bounce, a colliding kick, and a paddle strike in human hands still return the object to holding: the system enters $\alpha$ and crosses $\alpha\kappa$ again.

Object manipulation lives in kratos. Juggling keeps akrateia and kratos together and may briefly enter total akrateia.
