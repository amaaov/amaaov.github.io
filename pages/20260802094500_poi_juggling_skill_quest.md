# Poi Juggling Skill Quest

Cumulative BN Charter with digit-locked rhythm strata and a plane-authorship track. Familiar cumulative grade bands, poi-specific difficulty, local practice log.

Independent programme. Unaffiliated with the World Juggling Federation. Grade-band letters borrow a familiar cumulative shell; tasks and difficulty are written for tethered poi.

## Charter

This programme grades technical toss juggling with poi. Continuous spinning, weaves, flowers, contact, and wraps remain available practice; they do not pass a juggling requirement on their own. Repeated throws and dwell form the sport core.

### Grades

- Entry–1C three-poi foundation
- 2A–2C advanced three + intermediate four
- 3A–3C advanced four + five acquisition
- 4A–4C five as peak; keep lower numbers mixed

### Foundation order

pendulum 3 companion → backward no-beat cascade 3 principal → wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) → backward + extra spin / sync relation 3 → wallplane halfshower (extra spin) (own pattern) → forward cascade 3 → forward synchronous cascade 3. Forward after backward. One-up 360 before 180.

### Digit lock (rhythm stratum)

Through 2C siteswap work under backward no-beat unless stated: digit 2 always spinning; digit 1 passed with spin. Those dwell semantics follow Bow’s public no-beat siteswap catalogue (see Sources).

### Wallplane dwell by object count

Odd counts (3, 5, 7): front-wall patterns in this quest (wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin), wallplane halfshower (extra spin), wallplane synchronous 5 (extra spin), and later odd front plane) use extra spin only. No-beat front plane on odd counts is not required.

Even counts (2, 4, 6, 8): front plane work is mostly one-hand pair control. four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin) may be no-beat or extra-spin; declare and hold. As even siteswaps grow, difficulty rises through hand and body position.

### Body throws: BC before BTB

backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) (BC — wallplane-friendly body throw across the back line (not fully blind BTB)) is front plane-first and less hard than fully blind behind-the-back (fully blind). Introduce backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) from wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) / wallplane halfshower (extra spin) before behind-the-back (fully blind). behind-the-back (fully blind) 1 (single blind throw) is easier than connected behind-the-back (fully blind) 3 / behind-the-back (fully blind) cascade. Halfshower behind-the-back (fully blind) declares low-pass, high-pass, or all-passes — all-passes is extreme.

### Selective limitation

Each rank sheet should state which freedoms are fixed, which stay open, and which transformations are under test. The sport object is narrower than all available poi practice. In this quest, cascade work for BN3 and F3 is side-plane; front-wall work uses WS3, WH3, 4WS-S, WS5, and other named front patterns. Side-plane four (4BN) stays no-beat. Odd wallplane = extra spin; even wallplane dwell is declared (see Wallplane dwell by object count).

## Faults & evidence

### General

Drop, collision, wrap, wrong catch region, uncontrolled stepping, incomplete turn, pause disguising a failed transition, failed qualify in/out.

### Modality

Undeclared backward↔forward or async↔sync conversion; missing/extra spin; hidden corrective orbit; accidental pendulum; easier modality than declared. Odd wallplane run as no-beat fails this quest. For even wallplane (4WS-S), undeclared or drifting no-beat↔extra-spin fails — both dwells are legal only when declared and held.

### Plane

Side planes = beside the body. Front wall = facing the camera wall. BN3 and F3 stay on the side planes; front-wall patterns are WS3, WH3, later 4WS-S and WS5 (and other named front patterns). Plane changes connect named side and front patterns by hand-driven break or by body-turn. Using the wrong method, or drifting without declaring it, fails. Missing the second camera angle on plane tasks fails. Odd front-wall destinations are extra-spin; even front-wall destinations declare dwell and do not inherit side-plane 4BN dwell automatically.

### Body throws

Labelling front plane backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) as behind-the-back (fully blind) when the catch was not blind fails honesty. behind-the-back (fully blind) must recover the named pattern. Halfshower behind-the-back (fully blind) without declaring low-pass, high-pass, or all-passes fails. Early plane turns expect crossing throws / backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB); requiring only behind-the-back (fully blind) there over-grades the soft path.

### Camera

Ordinary runs: fixed frontal-oblique, full body and props visible. Plane tasks: frontal-oblique + lateral, no moving camera. Original speed; no hidden cuts inside a requirement; equipment declared (length, diameter, head mass, swivel, differences).

## Entry — Accept the Charter

### Objectives

- backward no-beat cascade 3: six throws (qualifying run).
- Clean collection of all three poi.

### Conditions

- No drops; no edits; heads, tethers, and hands visible.
- No accidental conversion into pendulum or forward cascade.

Entry opens the quest log. backward no-beat cascade 3 is the main foundation; pendulum 3 and wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) (extra spin) arrive as companions in 1A.

## 1A — Foundational Three

### Runs

- backward no-beat cascade 3: 30 throws.
- backward no-beat cascade 3: 12 throws beginning from each launch hand.
- pendulum 3: 20 throws.
- wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin): 12 throws.

### Elementary throws

- One high throw from each hand inside backward no-beat cascade 3.
- One over-the-top throw from each side.
- One under-arm throw from each side.
- Clean collection from backward no-beat cascade 3 and from wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin).

### Plane task

- Keep backward no-beat cascade 3 on the side planes (beside you). Keep wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) facing the front wall — extra spin, not no-beat.
- If the pattern drifts from side to front (or reverse) without you meaning to, the run fails.

Short-form descriptor allowed: objects, siteswap, modality, body-relative class, throw count, equipment.

## 1B — Extra Spin & 360

### Runs

- backward no-beat cascade 3: 50 throws.
- backward + extra spin / sync relation 3: 20 throws.
- wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin): 25 throws.

### Connections

- backward no-beat cascade 3 → backward + extra spin / sync relation 3 → backward no-beat cascade 3, three times.
- Same connection from the opposite hand.

### Body throws

- One front plane backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) from each side (from wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin)).
- Three connected front plane backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) from wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin).
- Three connected overhead throws.
- Three connected under-arm throws.

### Pirouettes

- One-up 360 from the right, return to backward no-beat cascade 3.
- One-up 360 from the left, return to backward no-beat cascade 3.

### Plane task

- Through the one-up 360s, return to backward no-beat cascade 3 on the side planes. Do not finish facing the front wall.
- Front-wall work at this grade stays on wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin).

backward no-beat cascade 3 stays beside you. Front wall = wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) / later wallplane halfshower (extra spin) (odd front plane = extra spin only). backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) first; behind-the-back (fully blind) is not required here.

## 1C — Upper-Basic Three

### Runs

- backward no-beat cascade 3: 100 throws.
- backward + extra spin / sync relation 3: 50 throws.
- wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin): 50 throws.
- forward cascade 3: one qualifying run (twice object count).

### Patterns

- wallplane halfshower (extra spin): six high throws from the right.
- wallplane halfshower (extra spin): six high throws from the left.
- siteswap 522: 12 throws in backward no-beat digit-locked modality (2 always spinning; 1 passed with spin).

### Body throws & pirouettes

- Six connected overhead throws.
- Six connected front plane backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) from wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) or wallplane halfshower (extra spin).
- Three-up 360 from backward no-beat cascade 3; opposite phase; then six-throw backward no-beat cascade 3 qualification.

### Plane task

- Facing the front wall, run wallplane halfshower (extra spin) as a real halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) with uneven highs — extra spin, not wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) renamed.
- Side-plane backward no-beat cascade 3 and front-wall wallplane halfshower (extra spin) must look like different patterns.

forward cascade 3 is acquisition here, not yet an endurance foundation. Rhythm stratum: siteswap 522 under backward no-beat frame. Connected backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) still front plane-first; behind-the-back (fully blind) cascade is later.

## 2A — Forward & Four Acquisition

### Three-poi runs

- forward cascade 3: 30 throws; 12 from each launch hand.
- wallplane halfshower (extra spin): 12 high throws from each side.

### Siteswaps (BN digit-locked unless stated)

- siteswap 423: 18 throws.
- siteswap 441: 18 throws.
- siteswap 522: 24 throws.

### Body throws

- One behind-the-back (fully blind) 1 from each side; recover backward no-beat cascade 3 or wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) as declared.

### Pirouettes

- One-up 180 accept-inversion: backward no-beat cascade 3 → forward cascade 3, each hand.
- One-up 180 accept-inversion: forward cascade 3 → backward no-beat cascade 3, each hand.
- Three-up 360 from backward no-beat cascade 3 and from forward cascade 3.

### Four-poi acquisition

- four-poi BN async fountain: eight throws, three successful runs.
- four-poi BN sync fountain: eight throws, three successful runs.
- Clean collection from each fountain.

### Plane task

- Keep the body roughly still. Hand-driven change between side-plane backward no-beat cascade 3 and front-wall wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) — both ways, without stopping. Both destinations must be clear.

Modality stratum opens for forward cascade 3 endurance; rhythm stratum continues under backward no-beat for listed siteswaps. First blind behind-the-back (fully blind) is singles (behind-the-back (fully blind) 1), not a cascade.

## 2B — Synchronous Forward & Corrected 180

### Runs & connections

- forward cascade 3: 50 throws; forward synchronous cascade 3: 12 throws; wallplane halfshower (extra spin): 20 high throws each side.
- forward cascade 3 → forward synchronous cascade 3 → forward cascade 3, three times.
- backward no-beat cascade 3 → forward cascade 3 → backward no-beat cascade 3 without pirouette (declared direction-changing transition).
- siteswap 531: 12 throws.

### Pirouettes

- Flat one-up 180 with prescribed backward restart, each hand.
- Overspin one-up 180 corrected to backward no-beat cascade 3, each hand.
- Two connected one-up 360s from each hand.

### Four poi

- four-poi BN async fountain / four-poi BN sync fountain: 20 throws each.
- 53: eight throws.
- Two-up async and sync 360; two-up async and sync 180 with declared destination modality.

### Plane tasks

- Body-turn change between side-plane backward no-beat cascade 3 and front-wall wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) without stopping — both ways. Crossing throws and front plane backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) are the expected aids; behind-the-back (fully blind) is allowed but harder. Land in the named pattern (backward no-beat cascade 3 or wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin)), not an unnamed mix.
- Same backward no-beat cascade 3↔wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) destinations via the 2A hand-driven path. The two mechanisms must look different on video.
- One coupled transition with declared approximate body and prop angles.

Plane work here connects side-plane backward no-beat cascade 3 with front-wall wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin) (extra spin). Prefer backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB) over behind-the-back (fully blind) for the soft path through the turn.

## 2C — Advanced Three & Established Four

### Three poi

- forward cascade 3: 100 throws; forward synchronous cascade 3: 30 throws.
- backward no-beat cascade 3 → backward + extra spin / sync relation 3 → forward cascade 3 → forward synchronous cascade 3 → forward cascade 3 → backward no-beat cascade 3.
- siteswap 423 → siteswap 441 → siteswap 522 → siteswap 531 returning to declared base.
- Three-up 360 linked to six backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB); to twelve overheads.
- Three connected behind-the-back (fully blind) (cascade fragment); recover declared backward no-beat cascade 3 or wallplane synchronous 3 (extra spin).
- Connected three-up 360s; three-up 180 accept / corrected; one-up 720.

### Four poi

- four-poi BN async fountain / four-poi BN sync fountain: 50 throws each.
- four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin): 16 throws; clean collection. Declare no-beat or extra-spin (even front plane — one-hand pair control).
- 53: 16; siteswap 534: 8; siteswap 7531: 8 throws. Keep declared hand and body positions honest as siteswap height grows.
- Four-up async/sync 360; two-up 180 natural and corrected.
- Four-poi plane task twice: body-turn vs hand-driven change between side-plane four-poi BN sync fountain and front-wall four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin). Declare four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin) dwell (no-beat or extra-spin).

Even front plane (four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin)): one-hand pairs; declare no-beat or extra-spin. Odd front plane stays extra-spin only. Even siteswaps get harder through hand and body positions.

## 3A — Four Mastery & Five Acquisition

### Maintenance & four

- forward synchronous cascade 3: 50 throws; one mixed three-poi sequence (backward no-beat/BXS/F/FS + 360 + corrected 180 + both plane classes).
- four-poi BN async fountain / four-poi BN sync fountain: 100 throws; four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin): 30 throws (declared no-beat or extra-spin); 53: 24; siteswap 534: 12; siteswap 7531: 16.
- Connected four-up 360s; four-up 180 natural; four-up 360 into 53.
- Six-throw backward no-beat cascade 3 with connected behind-the-back (fully blind) cascade; clean recovery.
- wallplane halfshower (extra spin) with declared behind-the-back (fully blind) low-pass OR high-pass (state which; six high throws that side).

### Five acquisition

- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: ten throws; three successful qualifying runs.
- wallplane synchronous 5 (extra spin): ten throws; clean collection (extra spin).
- Retain declared plane system for ten throws; no rescue via unintended diagonal.

## 3B — Forward Four & Controlled Five

### Objectives

- Corrected three-up 180 both phases; three-up 720 to backward no-beat cascade 3; wheel→wall→opposite-wheel connection.
- four-poi BN async / sync fountain: 150; four-poi wallplane sync fountain (even; declare no-beat or extra-spin): 50 (declared dwell); four-poi forward async fountain: 12; four-poi forward sync fountain: 8; 53: 32; siteswap 534: 16.
- four-poi backward no-beat fountain ↔ four-poi forward fountain connections; four-up 360 + twenty-throw qualify; corrected four-up 180.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: 20; wallplane synchronous 5 (extra spin): 20; five-poi backward + extra spin: 10; three-up and one-up 360s; five-poi halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) five highs each side.
- five-poi pendulum: ten throws; clean collection.
- Five-poi orientation-preserving pirouette recovering original planes in space.

## 3C — Stable Five

### Objectives

- One connected three-poi sequence with corrected 180, 360, siteswap, both plane transitions.
- four-poi forward async fountain: 30; four-poi forward sync fountain: 12; 53: 50; siteswap 534: 24; siteswap 7531: 16; connected four-up 360s; corrected four-up 180.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: 50; wallplane synchronous 5 (extra spin): 40; five-poi pendulum: 20; five-poi backward + extra spin: 20; siteswap 744: 6; halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) ten highs (≥4/side); phase 360s; five-poi backward no-beat cascade↔five-poi backward + extra spin; five-poi backward no-beat cascade↔siteswap 744.
- One five-poi controlled plane break and recover without collecting.

## 4A — Five Technical Control

### Objectives

- One 3C-level three-poi and four-poi combination; four-poi forward async fountain: 50; four-up 360→corrected 180.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: 100; five-poi backward + extra spin: 30; siteswap 744: 12; halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) ten highs each side.
- Three-up 360s; connected three-up 360s; flat/overspin one-up 180 to five-poi backward no-beat cascade each hand; five-up 360 + qualify.
- Five-poi body-turn between a declared side pattern and a declared front pattern (name both in advance).

## 4B — Five Modality & Transitions

### Objectives

- Prescribed three- and four-poi maintenance sequences.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: 150; five-poi backward + extra spin: 50; five-poi forward cascade qualify; siteswap 744: 18; halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) 15 highs each side.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade → five-poi backward + extra spin → siteswap 744 → five-poi backward no-beat cascade; halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) loop; five-poi backward no-beat cascade ↔ five-poi forward cascade with declared direction changes.
- Connected three-up 360s; three-up 180 accept both ways; corrected three-up 180; connected five-up 360s.
- Same five-poi side↔front destinations via body-turn and via hand-driven break — mechanisms must look different.

## 4C — Highest Attestation

### Objectives

- Prescribed three-poi mega-combination (patterns, siteswap, 360, corrected 180, backcross (wallplane-first; not fully blind BTB), optional declared halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) all-passes behind-the-back (fully blind), all three transition classes).
- four-poi forward async fountain: 100; four-poi forward sync fountain: 50; 53: 100; advanced four siteswap sequence; connected four-up 360s + corrected 180.
- five-poi backward no-beat cascade: 200; five-poi backward + extra spin: 100; five-poi forward cascade: 30; siteswap 744: 24; halfshower (uneven-height cascade family) 20 highs each side.
- Connected three-up / five-up 360s; five-up 180 accept and corrected; five-up two-stage 720.
- Long five connections including a full technical combination and clean collection.
- One destination via all three transition mechanisms with identical entry/exit patterns.

Highest defined grade in this programme version. Self-tracked practice log still applies on this page; peer review remains the proposed shell in the Charter.
